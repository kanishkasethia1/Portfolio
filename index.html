About

A little about me

Kanishka is a Singapore-based marketing professional currently pursuing her MSc in Management at Singapore Management University. She is targeting roles in media planning, strategy, client servicing, and campaign management — positions where sharp thinking, structured execution, and strong stakeholder communication matter.

She is especially drawn to work that combines media planning, client servicing, campaign strategy, performance marketing, and consulting. She understands how to translate a client brief into a media plan, how to track and optimise a campaign in flight, and how to communicate results to stakeholders clearly. Her work spans strategy decks, audience segmentation, channel planning, competitive benchmarking, and campaign analytics — always with a focus on what is actionable and measurable.

She thrives in client-facing and cross-functional roles — comfortable owning a brief, building the plan, and seeing it through to delivery. Her strengths lie in structured thinking, clear communication, media and campaign execution, and data-backed decision making — the combination that makes a strong media executive, strategy consultant, or account manager.

Values

How I Work

The mindset and working style I bring to every media, strategy, or client brief.

🧠
Structured Thinker

I naturally bring structure to ambiguity — whether that's a client brief, a campaign plan, a media schedule, or a market analysis. I translate scattered inputs into clear frameworks, prioritised plans, and organised thinking that teams and clients can act on. This shows up in every brief, plan, and client deliverable I produce.

📊
Data-Driven and Insight-Led

I use data to sharpen decisions, not just report on them. From campaign performance dashboards to market sizing to competitor benchmarks, I focus on what the numbers actually mean and what should change because of them. In media planning and performance roles, this shows up as better channel allocation, tighter targeting, and clearer reporting for clients.

🎯
Media Planning and Channel Strategy

Media planning is where strategy meets execution — and it's where I want to build my career. I understand how to move from a business objective to an audience strategy to a channel plan, and how each media decision connects to a measurable outcome. I think in terms of reach, frequency, funnel stage, and ROI — not just creatives and platforms.

🤝
Client and Stakeholder Minded

Client servicing is not just about delivery — it's about trust. I think from the client's perspective first: what do they need to feel confident, informed, and well-managed? Whether I'm preparing a campaign brief, presenting results, or managing a timeline, I focus on clarity, reliability, and communication. I want clients to feel that things are in hand.

⚡
Strong on Execution

Strategy without execution is just a document. I care deeply about follow-through — timelines, trackers, campaign coordination, vendor management, and the operational discipline that separates good plans from actual results. At Enfactum, I delivered 15 brand activation events. At Signify, I built dashboards that cut prospecting time by 70%. Execution is where I prove the thinking.

🔭
Curious Across Functions

The best marketing professionals understand more than one function — they connect strategy to media, media to data, and data to client outcomes. I actively seek roles that sit at that intersection. Whether it's a media planning role that requires analytical thinking, or a client executive role that requires strategic input, I bring the range and the curiosity to contribute across the brief.
