About

A little about me

Kanishka is a Singapore-based marketing, strategy, and analytics professional currently pursuing her MSc in Management at Singapore Management University. Her experience spans dashboards, go-to-market support, campaign coordination, market and competitor analysis, customer journey projects, and consulting-style business problem solving.

She is especially interested in work that combines media planning, client servicing, analytics, strategic thinking, and execution. She enjoys understanding business goals, translating them into structured plans, and building work that is both insight-led and practical. Whether she is working on dashboards, strategy decks, campaign support, engagement calendars, market analysis, or customer journey recommendations, she focuses on making information clearer, decisions sharper, and execution more effective.

She is drawn to roles where she can work across teams, support stakeholders and clients, and contribute to both the thinking and the delivery side of marketing. Her strengths lie in combining analytical ability, communication, creativity, and detail orientation — helping transform messy business challenges into clear recommendations, useful frameworks, and real outcomes.

Values

What Defines Me

More than skills — these are the ways I think, work, and approach problems.

🧠
Structured Thinker

One of the things that defines me most is the way I naturally bring structure to ambiguity. I enjoy taking information that feels broad, scattered, or messy — whether it's campaign inputs, business context, market signals, stakeholder requests, or raw data — and turning it into something clearer and more actionable. I like creating frameworks, priorities, and organised thinking that help people move from confusion to direction.

📊
Data-Driven and Insight-Led

I'm naturally drawn to dashboards, analysis, research, reporting, and patterns in information. But what defines me is not just an interest in data itself — it's the need to understand what that data is actually saying. I enjoy looking beyond surface-level numbers, identifying what matters, and translating findings into recommendations, sharper decisions, and clearer next steps.

🎯
Drawn to Media Planning and Business Context

I'm especially interested in the thinking behind marketing and media — how objectives are translated into plans, how audiences are understood, how channels support business goals, and how strategy connects to execution. What defines me here is that I'm not only interested in the output, but in the logic behind it: why something is being done, who it is for, and how it supports the bigger business picture.

🤝
Client and Stakeholder Minded

I often think about work from the perspective of the people who will use it, review it, present it, or act on it. Whether I'm working on strategy decks, dashboards, campaign support, recommendations, brochures, or research summaries, I care about clarity, usefulness, and communication. I like making work that not only looks organised, but is genuinely easier for teams, clients, and stakeholders to understand and act on.

⚡
Strong on Execution

What also defines me is that I care about execution just as much as ideas. I like structure, follow-through, timelines, and the discipline required to move work from concept to delivery. Whether it's campaign coordination, project support, engagement planning, timeline management, or stakeholder materials, I enjoy being involved in the side of work that makes things tangible, organised, and ready to move.

🔭
Curious Across Functions

More than anything, I'm defined by the fact that I enjoy working across functions rather than staying inside one narrow lane. I'm interested in the overlap between strategy, analytics, media, client servicing, business storytelling, and execution. I enjoy roles where I can think critically, learn quickly, and contribute in ways that connect insight with action and planning with delivery.
